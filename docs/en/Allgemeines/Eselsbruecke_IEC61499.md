# Mnemonic: IEC 61499

How can you reliably remember the number of the standard **IEC 61499** (Function Blocks for Industrial-Process Measurement and Control Systems) – the standard on which
4diac/FORTE, and thus this entire project, is based?
Answer: with a tractor.
## The mnemonic in one sentence

> **Case IH 1455** – everyone knows it. A 6-cylinder tractor. You already have the **6** and the
> **14** memorized. Now you just have to change the **55**
> to a **99** – and there you have **61499**.

## Step by step

| Component | Origin | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **6** | Case IH 1455 is a **6-cylinder** tractor | `6 _ _ _ _` |
| **14** | The first two digits of **14**55 | `6 1 4 _ _` |
| **99** | The **55** at the end of 1455 becomes **99** | `6 1 4 9 9` |

Combined, this gives: **6 · 14 · 99 → 61499**

## The trick with 55 → 99

The only step that isn't "easy" is converting the
last digits **55 → 99**. Two images that can help:

- **Mathematically:** 55 + 44 = 99 (the "44" can be thought of as rounding up the horsepower of the
tractor, if that helps).

- **Figuratively:** The two fives of the tractor type "grow" in your mind into two
nines – `1455` becomes `1499`.

The important thing is that the final impression is: **the last two digits of the
standard are NOT 55, but 99.**

It's a shame, really: The IHC plant closed in 1997 – if it had closed in 1999, the **99** could be derived directly from the year of closure, instead of having to artificially construct it from the 55.

## The complete chain of reasoning

1. I'm thinking of a **Case IH 1455**.
2. It has **6 cylinders** → first digit: **6**
3. The rest of the model name begins with **14** → next digits: **1**, **4**
4. The **55** at the end becomes **99** → last digits: **9**, **9**
5. Together: **6 – 1 – 4 – 9 – 9** = **IEC 61499**

## Why this works

Mnemonic devices work best when they link an abstract fact (a
5-digit standard number) to a concrete, vivid, and emotionally resonant piece of knowledge (a familiar tractor). The Case IH 1455 is exactly such an anchor: easily imaginable, unmistakable, and even provides three of the five digits (1, 4, 5) plus an additional fact (6 cylinders) free of charge. The only "artificial" step – 55 becomes 99 – is deliberately kept small so that the bridge doesn't become too unstable.

## Summary for Memorization

> **Case IH 1455, 6 cylinders. Turn 55 into 99. IEC 61499.**
