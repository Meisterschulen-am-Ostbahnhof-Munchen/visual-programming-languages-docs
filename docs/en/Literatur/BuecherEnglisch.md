# Books in English
## IEC 61499 Function Blocks for Embedded and Distributed Control Systems Design
### About the Book
This book is considered the definitive English-language standard work on the IEC 61499 standard. It examines the paradigm shift from centralized PLC control to distributed, component-based automation. The author provides a thorough introduction to the architectural model, execution semantics, and service interface function blocks. It is an invaluable resource for software architects and control engineers designing the next generation of industrial IoT (IIoT) systems.
### About the Author
**Valeriy Vyatkin** is a professor of computer science in industrial automation. With professorships at Aalto University (Finland) and Luleå University of Technology (Sweden), he is a leading academic voice in the advancement of intelligent, distributed automation. His work was crucial in demonstrating how formal methods and distributed intelligence can make automation systems more flexible and reliable.

* [Amazon](https://www.amazon.de/Function-Embedded-Distributed-Control-Systems/dp/1936007932)

## Distributed Control Applications: Guidelines, Design Patterns, and Application Examples with the IEC 61499

### About the Book

This practical guide focuses on the application layer of IEC 61499. It introduces design patterns for distributed control, such as monitoring, fault tolerance, and dynamic reconfiguration. Rich in real-world examples, the book provides clear guidelines for structuring large, distributed applications to ensure maintainability and performance.

### About the Authors
**Alois Zoitl** is Professor of Cyber-Physical Systems at Johannes Kepler University (JKU) Linz. He is one of the founding fathers of the Eclipse 4diac project and has dedicated his career to developing high-quality open-source tools for IEC 61499 to make it accessible to industry.

Thomas Strasser is a Senior Scientist at AIT (Austrian Institute of Technology), specializing in smart grids and distributed energy systems, where he applies IEC 61499 concepts to modernize power grids.

* [Amazon](https://www.amazon.de/Distributed-Control-Applications-Application-Information/dp/1138892955)]

## Modelling Control Systems Using IEC 61499

### About the Book

This book provides an in-depth look at the modeling aspects of industrial control systems using the IEC 61499 standard. It emphasizes the importance of a systematic modeling approach to managing the complexity of modern, networked automation systems. This book covers the transition from requirements to formal models and on to executable code, highlighting the visual nature of the standard.

### About the Authors
**Robert W. Lewis** is a veteran in the field of PLC programming and industrial software standards. Together with **Alois Zoitl**, he combines decades of industry experience with academic rigor to offer a comprehensive view of how modeling can solve complex technical challenges in automation.

* [Amazon](https://www.amazon.de/Modelling-Control-Systems-Using-Engineering/dp/1849197601)]
