**The Hardware: The Robust Heart of Your Machine Control**

The logiBUS hardware forms the physical foundation for bringing your digitally created ideas to life. Here, the control logics designed in the graphical programming environment are brought to life and executed directly on the machine. Each of our controllers is the link between the digital world of your application and the real world of actuators and sensors in the field.

---

**Built for Practical Use**

Based on the **proven and award-winning ISOmaX technology**, our controllers are specifically designed for the demanding conditions of agriculture. They are characterized by their outstanding **robustness, reliability, and flexibility**. Whether you want to implement a simple control system for a small implement or develop complex automation for a specialized machine – our hardware offers the necessary performance and stability.
